# Dashboard image assets

world_market_coverage.png is a fixed geographic coverage image using Natural Earth 1:110m country geometry. Highlighted countries are the 12 selected practice markets. Colour identifies the practice region, not sales intensity. This image does not respond to workbook filters. The accompanying regional sales numbers are live formulas. Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/ and https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector . The country geometry is public-domain Natural Earth data.

sports_banner.png is an AI-generated fictional sports product concept image, not an actual PUMA catalogue product or campaign. It is used decoratively in the learning dashboard. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: a wide studio photograph of a black/red running shoe, folded charcoal training top and sports bag on a pale-grey backdrop with empty space on the left; no logos, text or watermark.

Images are supplied so you can reproduce the dashboard. Native Excel charts and formulas own the numbers.
